# Operator Portal Guide — Rathayatra Call Center

This guide details caller workflows and visitor check-in procedures for Contact Operators.

---

## 1. Login to Portal

1. Go to `/admin/operator` in your browser.
2. Sign in with the credentials (email and password) provided by the administrator.
3. Upon authentication, you will access the Operator Portal.

---

## 2. Call Queue & Status Workflow

The portal lists all registrations assigned to you.

### Calling Statuses
Every contact has one of the following statuses:
- **Pending** (default): Yet to be called.
- **Coming**: Confirmed they are attending.
- **Not Coming**: Confirmed they will not attend.
- **Callback Required**: Line busy, requested call later, or did not answer.

### Taking Actions
1. Select a card. Tap the **Call (+91...)** button to dial directly from your device.
2. Alternatively, tap the **WhatsApp** button to open a direct messaging window pre-filled with the contact's number.
3. After the conversation, update the status dropdown on the card.
4. Click **Add Notes / Edit Notes** to log remarks (e.g. "Coming with 3 friends", "Requires transport").

---

## 3. Operator Visitor Check-In

If you are serving at the registration desk during the festival, click **Visitor Check-In** in the top navigation header:
- **Lookup Scoping**: You can search for visitors by Registration ID, Phone, or Name.
- **Operator Limitation**: Security policies allow you to search and check in **only** guests that were assigned to you.
- **Approve**: Confirm guest identity, view volunteer slots or dinner details, add gate remarks, and click "Approve Visit".
- **Status Checks**: If they are already checked in, the system alerts you and disables check-in buttons to prevent duplicate entries.
